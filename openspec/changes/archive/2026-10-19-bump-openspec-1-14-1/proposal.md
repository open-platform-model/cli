## Why

The cli installs OpenSpec 1.12.0 locally and in CI, while the other repos move to 1.14.1. The generated OpenSpec skills in `.claude/skills/` are at `generatedBy: "1.12.0"`. The bump aligns the cli with the rest of the workspace.

`--strict` leaves `task openspec:check` for now. Version 1.14.1 reports a requirement longer than 500 characters as a WARNING. `--strict` fails on any warning, and no flag turns this one check off. The cli has 215 such requirements in its main specs. A later change rewrites them and puts `--strict` back.

## What Changes

- **Pin.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `.github/workflows/pr.yml` and `Taskfile.yml` (`openspec:install`) install `@fission-ai/openspec@1.14.1`.
- **Gate.** `task openspec:check` runs `openspec validate --all --no-interactive`. Its `desc`, the line in `AGENTS.md` and the line in `openspec/config.yaml` say that `--strict` is off until the rewrite lands.
- **Skills.** The eleven generated `openspec-*` skills are regenerated by 1.14.1 (`generatedBy: "1.14.1"`). The local edit of `openspec-verify-change` (Cleanliness dimension, Task Hygiene block, naming lines) is merged in by a three-way merge. `openspec-cleanup-change` is hand-written and stays as it is.

Release class: none. CI, task and skill files only; the `opm` binary does not change.
